# RxDrop - Account Deletion Request

**App:** RxDrop

**Developer:** EPIC MEDICAL PTE. LTD.

**Last Updated:** May 16, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your RxDrop account and the associated data we hold about you. It applies to the RxDrop mobile application published on Google Play by EPIC MEDICAL PTE. LTD.

## Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your RxDrop account in any of the following ways.

### Option 1: From within the RxDrop app (recommended)

1. Open the RxDrop app and sign in with your account.
2. Tap the **Settings** icon.
3. Under the **Account** section, tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account and associated data will be deleted within **30 days** of confirmation.

### Option 2: Submit a request through the web form

**→ [Submit an account deletion request](https://forms.gle/4LCfA59opMwisxeD6)**

Use this option if you no longer have access to the app. The form will ask for:

- The email address associated with your RxDrop account
- Confirmation that you want your account and data deleted

What happens next:

1. We will verify your identity using the email address you submit.
2. You will receive an email confirmation of receipt within **7 business days**.
3. Your account and associated data will be deleted within **30 days** of verification.
4. You will receive a final email confirmation once the deletion is complete.

### Option 3: Contact us by email

If you cannot use the form, you may also email **vision@humblebee.ai** from the email address associated with your RxDrop account, using the subject line **"RxDrop Account Deletion Request"**.

## What Data We Store

RxDrop is designed to minimize the data we collect and store. We only store:

- **Account information** — your email address and authentication credentials (password hash, authentication tokens) used to sign you in.
- **Saved flow rate results** — if you choose to save a measurement by tapping the **Save** button in the app, the resulting flow rate value is stored against your account. Measurements you do not explicitly save are discarded.

## Data That Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently deleted from our systems:

- Your account information (email address, password hash, authentication tokens)
- All saved flow rate results linked to your account

## Backups

Account deletion takes effect across our active systems within 30 days. Residual copies of deleted data may persist in our cloud provider's encrypted backup systems for a short additional period (typically up to 30 days) before being overwritten in the normal course of backup rotation.

## Locally Stored Data

Some data is stored locally on your device (authentication tokens, user preferences, and any flow rate results you chose to save). This data is **not** deleted by our account deletion process. To remove locally stored data, **uninstall the RxDrop app** from your device.
